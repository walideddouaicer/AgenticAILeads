FAQ

Fair questions.

What counts as a "qualified" meeting?

Before we start, we define your ideal client together — industry, company size, roles they hire for, geography. A meeting only counts if the prospect matches that profile and showed up. If they don't match or don't show, you pay nothing.

Why is the first meeting free?

Because you have no reason to trust us yet, and we'd rather prove it than pitch it. The free meeting is our audition. If it's not worth your time, we part ways and you've lost nothing.

How is this different from buying a lead list?

A list is names. We deliver conversations — a decision-maker who knows who you are, has a reason to talk, and agreed to a time. The difference is everything that happens between the list and the calendar, which is exactly the part you don't have time to do.

Will this damage my agency's reputation?

The opposite of mass outreach: every message is individually personalized to a real hiring signal, sent at human volume. Nothing goes out without your approval of the targeting and messaging first.

What do you need from us to start?

One 30-minute call to define your ideal client, and your calendar availability. We handle everything else.
