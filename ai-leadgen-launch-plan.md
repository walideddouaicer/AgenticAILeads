# AI Lead Generation — $0 Launch Plan
**Walid Eddouaicer · Started July 3, 2026 · Full-time · US + Europe**

## The strategy in one paragraph

You have $0 and 6+ hours/day. Money buys sending infrastructure; time buys manual precision. So for the first 30 days you ARE the AI lead gen system, done by hand on LinkedIn and free tools. You sell pay-per-result (no risk for the client, no credibility needed from you). The first client's first payment (~$300–1,500) funds the real stack — domains, Instantly, Apollo — and from there you scale into retainers.

**Niche (picked for you): recruitment and marketing agencies, 2–20 employees.**
Why: they already buy leads so no education needed, they live on LinkedIn so you can reach them free, their deal sizes justify $100–275/meeting, and there are tens of thousands of them across the US/UK/EU. Recruitment agencies especially — one placed candidate is worth $15–25k to them, so a booked meeting has obvious value.

## The offer

> "I book qualified sales meetings for recruitment/marketing agencies. You pay only per meeting that actually happens: first meeting free, then $150 per showed meeting. No retainer, no contract."

Move to $1,500/mo base + $100/meeting after 2–3 clients (industry-standard starter structure).

## TONIGHT (before midnight) — ~3 hours

1. **LinkedIn profile rewrite (45 min).** Headline: "I book qualified sales meetings for recruitment agencies — pay per meeting, not promises." Banner via Canva free. About section = the offer above + how you work (signal-based targeting, personalized outreach, no spam).
2. **Free accounts (30 min).** Apollo (free: 100+ email credits/mo), Hunter.io (free: 25 searches/mo), HubSpot CRM (free), Google Sheet as pipeline tracker.
3. **Define the ICP precisely (30 min).** Recruitment agencies, 2–20 employees, US/UK/FR, founder or MD identifiable on LinkedIn, active in last 30 days (posting = they respond to DMs).
4. **Build List #1 (60 min).** 50 agencies via LinkedIn search + Apollo free filters. Columns: company, founder name, LinkedIn URL, email (Hunter/Apollo), one personalization hook (recent post, new job listing they're recruiting for, funding, market they specialize in).
5. **Write 2 DM templates + 1 email template (30 min).** Rule from the 2026 benchmark data: reference a specific signal ("saw you're hiring for 3 sales roles at client X...") — signal-based messages get 15–25% replies vs 3.4% generic.

**Do NOT tonight:** buy domains you can't afford, send from your personal Gmail in bulk, or pitch anyone before the profile is rewritten.

## Days 2–7 — prove the motion (target: 250 touches)

- 25–30 LinkedIn connection requests/day with a note (LinkedIn's practical limit ~100–200/week — stay under it).
- 10–15 highly personalized manual emails/day from a properly set up free Gmail — every one hand-written, this volume is safe.
- Follow up every prospect at day 3 and day 7 (most replies come from follow-ups).
- Track everything in the sheet: sent → replied → call booked.
- Free proof-of-work asset: for 5 best prospects, build them a free mini lead list (10 prospects for THEIR business) and send it unsolicited. "Here's 10 companies hiring in your niche right now, with contacts. Want 50 more per week?" This is the strongest $0 sales weapon you have.

**Week 1 realistic math:** 250 touches × ~8% reply ≈ 20 conversations ≈ 2–4 sales calls.

## Days 8–30 — land client #1

- Keep daily volume constant (this is the job now: 3h prospecting/list building, 2h outreach, 1h follow-ups + calls).
- On sales calls: sell the pilot. "First meeting I book you is free. Then $150/showed meeting. Cancel anytime." Close rate on this offer is high because their risk is zero.
- Deliver client #1 manually: their ICP list built with your free credits, outreach sent through THEIR inbox or LinkedIn (you write, they send — bypasses your infrastructure gap entirely and their domain is already warm).
- Realistic: first paying client between day 20–45.

## Days 30–60 — reinvest and industrialize

First ~$300–500 earned buys, in order:
1. 2–3 lookalike domains ($12/ea) + 2–4 Google Workspace inboxes (~$7/ea/mo) → **start warming immediately, 4–6 weeks at 5–10 emails/day ramping up** (2026 Gmail rules: permanent rejections for violators, complaints under 0.1%)
2. Instantly ($37/mo) or Smartlead ($39/mo) — automated sending + warmup
3. Apollo paid ($49–99/mo) — real list volume
4. Clay ($149/mo) — only after client #2; this is what makes it an *AI* lead gen agency (auto-enrichment, signal detection, AI personalization at scale)

By day 60: 2–3 clients, ~$1,000–4,000/mo, warmed infrastructure, transition to $1,500 base + $100/meeting.

## Rules that keep you alive

- Deliverability is the business. Never bulk-send from an unwarmed domain or your main domain.
- Sell the meeting, not the tech. Nobody pays for "AI" — they pay for booked calls.
- One niche until $5k/mo. Switching niches resets everything.
- Every reply, even "no," gets answered within 2 hours during US/EU business hours.
